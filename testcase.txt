# Application Pipeline Validation Test Cases
**Project:** Product Intelligence RAG Pipeline  
**Purpose:** Validate the application pipeline using test cases specifically designed for the ingested dataset.

---

## TC01: Direct Product Lookup

**Objective:** Validate product retrieval and factual answer generation.

**Test Query:**

What are the key features, price, and rating of the Sony WH-CH720N?

**Expected Answer:**

- **Product:** Sony WH-CH720N
- **Price:** ₹7,990
- **Rating:** 4.5
- **Key Features:**
  - Active Noise Cancellation (ANC)
  - Up to 35-hour battery life
  - Bluetooth 5.3
  - Lightweight design

**Validation Criteria:**
- Retrieves the correct product information.
- Returns the expected price and rating.
- Includes the key features supported by the ingested catalogue.
