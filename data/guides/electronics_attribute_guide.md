# Electronics Attribute Guide

## Earbuds and Headphones

When comparing audio products, consider:

- Price
- Battery life
- Active Noise Cancellation (ANC)
- Bluetooth version
- Driver size
- Codec
- IP rating
- Customer rating

## Battery Life

"Total battery life" normally includes the charging case for
true wireless earbuds.

"Earbud-only battery" refers to playback before the earbuds
need to be returned to the charging case.

## ANC

ANC means Active Noise Cancellation.

Do not treat ENC as equivalent to ANC.

ANC actively reduces external noise.

ENC generally focuses on microphone and call-noise handling.

## Recommendation Rule

A product must satisfy explicit hard constraints such as
maximum price before softer preferences such as rating
are considered.
