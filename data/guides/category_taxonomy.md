# ShopSmart India Category Taxonomy

## Electronics

Relevant attributes:

- Battery life
- Charging time
- Bluetooth version
- Driver size
- ANC/noise cancellation
- Codec
- IP rating
- Warranty

## Fashion

Relevant attributes:

- Size
- Fit
- Material
- Color
- Sleeve length
- Closure
- Wash care
- Occasion

## Home & Kitchen

Relevant attributes:

- Capacity
- Dimensions
- Power/wattage
- Material
- Safety features
- Cleaning method
- Warranty

## Beauty

Relevant attributes:

- Ingredients
- SPF
- Finish
- Shade
- Volume
- Usage instructions

## Books

Relevant attributes:

- Author
- Format
- Publisher
- Language
- Page count
- Subject
- Edition

## Catalogue Rule

Product IDs are the stable identifiers used for catalogue traceability.

Prices are listed in Indian rupees (INR) and may change.

Answers should rely on the retrieved catalogue record.
