# ShopSmart India Seller Onboarding Guide

## Electronics Image Requirements

Electronics listings should include:

1. One clear front product image.
2. One rear or side image where ports, controls, or important physical details are visible.
3. Images must be sharp enough to identify the product.
4. Images must not contain unrelated products or misleading claims.
5. Images should represent the exact model being sold.

## Catalogue Information

Sellers must provide accurate:

- Product name
- Brand
- Model or product ID
- Price
- Specifications
- Warranty information where applicable
- Seller ID

## Fashion Listings

Fashion listings should provide:

- Size information
- Fit
- Material
- Color
- Care instructions

## Seller Responsibility

Sellers are responsible for accurate catalogue data.

Misleading specifications or images can lead to listing review or removal.
