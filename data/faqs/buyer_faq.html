ShopSmart India - Buyer FAQ

General Ordering & Account

How can I track my order?

Open your ShopSmart India orders page and select the relevant order to view the latest tracking information. You will receive email notifications at each stage of delivery - order confirmation, dispatch, in-transit, and delivery.

What payment methods does ShopSmart India accept?

We accept multiple payment options including credit cards, debit cards, net banking, UPI, digital wallets (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), and EMI options for purchases above ₹10,000. All transactions are secure and encrypted.

How do I create an account on ShopSmart India?

Visit our website or app, click on "Sign Up", and enter your email address or phone number. Verify your contact information and set a password. You can also sign up using your Google or Facebook account for quick registration.

Is my personal information safe?

Yes, ShopSmart India uses industry-standard SSL encryption and follows all data protection regulations. We never share your personal information with third parties without your consent.

Can I change my order after placing it?

You can modify your order within 30 minutes of placing it if it hasn't entered the warehouse for processing. Go to your orders page and select "Edit Order". After this window, you'll need to cancel and create a new order.

How do I use a discount or promo code?

During checkout, there's a field for "Promo Code" or "Coupon Code". Enter the code and click "Apply". The discount will be reflected in your order total. Ensure the code meets all eligibility criteria before applying.

Shipping & Delivery

What are the shipping charges?

ShopSmart India offers free shipping on orders above ₹500. For orders below ₹500, a flat shipping charge of ₹40-100 applies depending on your location. Expedited delivery options are available for an additional fee.

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 3-7 business days in metro cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Chennai) and 7-10 business days for other locations. Express delivery (1-2 days) is available in select areas for an additional charge.

Can I change my delivery address?

You can change the delivery address within 6 hours of placing your order, provided it hasn't been dispatched. Go to your order details and click "Edit Address". If the order is already dispatched, contact our customer service team.

What should I do if my package is damaged?

Report the damage immediately upon receipt. Take photos of the damaged packaging and product, then contact our support team with your order ID and images. We'll arrange a replacement or refund within 5-7 business days.

Do you ship internationally?

Currently, ShopSmart India ships only within India. We are expanding our international shipping to select countries. For updates, check our shipping policy page.

Home & Kitchen

Are cookware items dishwasher safe?

Most modern cookware sets are dishwasher safe, but hand washing is recommended for longevity. Always check the product specifications. Non-stick items especially benefit from hand washing to preserve the coating.

What is the difference between stainless steel and non-stick cookware?

Stainless steel cookware is durable and versatile but requires oil for non-stick cooking. Non-stick cookware uses a coating (usually PTFE or ceramic) for easy cooking and cleanup but has a limited lifespan. Choose based on your cooking style and maintenance preference.

Can I use induction cooktops with all cookware?

No, cookware must be magnetic for induction cooking. Most stainless steel and cast iron work, but aluminum and copper don't. Product descriptions specify if items are "induction compatible". Our range includes dedicated induction cookware sets.

What warranty do kitchen appliances come with?

Kitchen appliances typically come with 1-2 year manufacturer warranty covering defects. Some items offer extended warranty options. Refer to product specifications for warranty duration. Service center contact information is provided with your purchase.

How do I maintain a water purifier?

Regular maintenance includes changing filters every 6-12 months (depending on water quality), cleaning the storage tank quarterly, and checking the TDS level monthly. Refer to your purifier's manual for specific maintenance schedule and instructions.

Are water purifiers like Kent RO effective for hard water?

Yes, RO (Reverse Osmosis) purifiers like Kent RO are highly effective for hard water, removing minerals and impurities. Our RO models include TDS adjustment features to maintain essential minerals. Check the product specifications for filtration stages and effectiveness data.

Are products cruelty-free and eco-friendly?

How do I check the expiry date of beauty products?

Are herbal and Ayurvedic products like Himalaya and Khadi safe?

Yes, reputable brands like Himalaya, Khadi, and Forest Essentials use natural ingredients and are dermatologically tested. However, natural doesn't always mean suitable for everyone. Those with sensitive skin should patch test first or consult a dermatologist.

Do you sell dermatologist-recommended brands?

Yes, we carry dermatologist-recommended brands like Cetaphil, Neutrogena, and L'Oreal Paris. These are specially formulated for sensitive skin and are trusted by skincare professionals. Check reviews and product descriptions for dermatologist endorsements.

Returns & Refunds

What is your return window?

Most items can be returned within 7-10 days of delivery. Electronics and books have a 10-day window, while clothing and accessories have 7 days. The return period starts from the delivery date, not the purchase date.

How do I initiate a return?

Go to your ShopSmart India account, select the order, and click "Return Item". Choose the reason for return and follow the instructions. You'll receive a return shipping label via email. Pack the item safely and schedule a pickup or drop at the nearest collection point.

Do I have to pay for return shipping?

ShopSmart India provides free return shipping for defective items or items not as described. For change-of-mind returns, a return shipping charge of ₹40-100 applies depending on your location. This is deducted from your refund.

How long does refund processing take?

Once we receive and inspect your return, refunds are processed within 5-7 business days. The refund is credited to your original payment method. If you used a credit card, it may take an additional 2-3 days to appear in your account.

Can I return an item without the original packaging?

Items should be returned in original or decent condition packaging. For defective items, packaging condition is not strictly required. For change-of-mind returns, items in original packaging get better refund consideration. Damaged or heavily used items may face deductions.

What if I receive the wrong item?

Contact our support team immediately with a photo of the received item and your order details. We'll arrange a replacement or refund without requiring you to return the incorrect item (in most cases). Your next correct item will be dispatched immediately.
