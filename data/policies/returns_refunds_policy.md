# ShopSmart India Returns and Refunds Policy

## Return Eligibility

Most eligible products may be returned within 7 days of delivery if the item is unused, in original condition, and includes original packaging and accessories.

Certain categories may have category-specific restrictions.

Hygiene-sensitive beauty products, opened consumables, personalized products, and products explicitly marked non-returnable may not qualify for a return.

## Sale Purchases

Items bought during a sale or promotional event are not automatically excluded from returns.

Return eligibility still depends on the product's stated return policy and condition requirements.

## Damaged or Incorrect Items

If an item arrives damaged, defective, or materially different from the order, the buyer should raise a support request promptly and provide order details and supporting photos when requested.

## Refunds

After an approved return is received and inspected, the refund is initiated to the original payment method where supported.

Bank or payment-provider processing time may vary.

## Exchanges

Exchanges are available only for eligible products and subject to stock availability.

## Non-returnable Examples

Products explicitly marked non-returnable, personalized items, opened hygiene-sensitive products, and certain consumables may be excluded.

## Policy Precedence

If a product page contains a specific return restriction, that product-level restriction takes precedence over the general policy.
